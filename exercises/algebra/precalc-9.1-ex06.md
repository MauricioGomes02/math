# Is (4, 0) a solution?

## Problem

Determine whether $(4, 0)$ is a solution of the system

$$
\begin{cases}
5x - y = 4 \\
x + 6y = 2
\end{cases}
$$

## My attempt

**First equation** ($5x - y = 4$):

$$
5(4) - 0 = 20 \neq 4
$$

**Answer.** $(4,\ 0)$ does not satisfy the first equation, so it is **not** a solution of the system
(one failed equation is enough).
